Straight answers

Office moving questions

How much do office movers cost in Dallas?

Curtis Specialized Moving & Storage publishes its Dallas rates: delivery and installation is $160 per hour for two installers and a truck (one-hour minimum), custom packing is $80 per hour per specialist, and a project manager is $90 per hour on moves with four or more movers. Full office moves are quoted after a walkthrough.

Do you assign a project manager to office moves?

Yes. On Dallas office moves with four or more movers or installers, Curtis assigns a project manager who plans the move with you, checks in on progress, and coordinates the crew leads from packing through final placement.

Does Curtis use subcontractors for office moves?

No. Every crew member on a Curtis Specialized Moving & Storage office move in Dallas is a Curtis employee — packers, drivers, installers and crew leads alike.

Can you install office furniture and hang art?

Yes. Curtis delivers, assembles and places office furniture and hangs art and mirrors as part of Dallas office moves. Interior design firms and showrooms use the same delivery and installation service for their commercial clients.

Can you store our furniture between offices?

Yes. Curtis receives, inspects and stores office furniture and fixtures in climate-controlled storage in Dallas, with each item catalogued, then delivers and installs everything when the new space is ready.

Are our furniture and equipment insured during the move?

Yes. Curtis Specialized Moving & Storage offers insurance and valuation coverage on office moves in Dallas, and the coverage for your move is defined in your client agreement before any work begins.

What areas do your office movers serve?

Curtis is based at 460 W Mockingbird Lane in Dallas and moves offices across Dallas–Fort Worth, including Downtown Dallas, Uptown, the Design District, Las Colinas, Addison, Plano, Frisco, Irving and Fort Worth. Long-distance and interstate relocations are also available.
