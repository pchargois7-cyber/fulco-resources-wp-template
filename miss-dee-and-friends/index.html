What happens on the bus

Make haircuts fun.

Every visit is personalized to your child. Wiggly toddlers, cautious first-timers and kids who need a little extra care are all regulars here.

Baby's first cut

Gentle, quick and celebrated. We go at baby's pace and you leave with a certificate-worthy moment.

Kids' cuts & styles

Trims, fades, bangs, braids and fresh looks for big kids and teens. Cartoons on, snacks optional.

Our specialty

Sensory-friendly cuts

Low lights, low noise, no rush. Built for kids with autism, Down syndrome and sensory sensitivities, one step at a time.

Meet Miss Dee

The friend behind the bus

Miss Dee grew up in Springfield, Missouri, and made Texas home after graduation to be close to family. In 2017, at 27, she noticed her town outside Houston had no children's salon at all, so she opened one. A few years later she put the whole thing on wheels.

Off the bus she's a dance therapist and yoga therapist, so movement and patience are baked into every visit. She knows how to meet a kid where they are, whether that means a dance break mid-cut or sitting quietly until they're ready.

Her favorite days end with a mommy-daughter date, and her favorite clients are the ones who walk up the bus steps nervous and walk down grinning.
